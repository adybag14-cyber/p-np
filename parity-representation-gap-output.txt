Verified identities for n >= 1:
  semantic classes        = 2
  minimum cube terms      = 2^n
  minimum cube literals   = n * 2^n
  full decision-tree nodes= 2^(n+1) - 1
  reduced OBDD nodes      = 2n + 1

Interpretation:
- Quotient cardinality alone does not control cube-cover size.
- Tree leaves cannot share equal residual parity states.
- The reduced OBDD merges all prefixes with equal accumulated parity.
