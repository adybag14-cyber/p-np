Interpretation
  MEDDLY getNodeCount() reports n reachable nonterminal nodes for the sum of n
  Boolean variables; getEdgeCount() reports 2n. With the terminal included,
  this agrees with the n+1 physical-node convention used in the Lean model.
  The diagram computes the exact integer population sum; modular output is an
  external cyclic-label interpretation of that exact sum.
