Interpretation:
  a complete nonimage proof is easy to check entry by entry
  but the raw proof has one entry for every candidate code
  the tiny exact experiments already need more certificate bits than table bits
  at b=n^2 the raw proof grows as n*2^(n^2)
  a P-versus-NP separation needs a succinct locally checkable replacement
