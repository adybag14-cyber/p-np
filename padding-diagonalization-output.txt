Interpretation:
  padding to the original runtime makes direct simulation degree one
  smaller polynomial padding does not fit the full deterministic history
  no fixed exponent simulates machines whose exponents are unbounded
  choosing a new hard language for every exponent does not yield one NP language
  the missing theorem must combine one fixed verifier with hardness against all P exponents
